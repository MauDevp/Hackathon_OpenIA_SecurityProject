# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

Expected behavior: be kind, assume good faith, give and accept constructive
feedback, focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in
SECURITY.md. Reports are handled confidentially.
